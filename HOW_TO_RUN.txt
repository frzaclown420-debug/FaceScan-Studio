ONE SCREEN. THAT IS THE WHOLE APP.

On Kali:

  cd ~/Desktop/facescan-studio
  source .venv/bin/activate
  export PYTHONPATH=.
  python3 app.py

Firefox: http://127.0.0.1:7860
Leave the terminal open.

1. Pick a preset 3D head (male / female / athletic / neutral)
2. Drop a frontal photo
3. Click Map photo onto head
4. Nudge sliders only if the face sits wrong
5. Click Make scan video
6. Play the MP4 fullscreen and scan with 2K HQ

No extra tabs. Scan settings (turn, blinks, light) are already baked in.
